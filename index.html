Lukas Trottner

I am an Assistant Professor and chair of Mathematical Statistics at the Institute for Stochastics and Applications at University of Stuttgart.

Before coming to Stuttgart, I was an Assistant Professor in Statistics at the University of Birmingham from September 2024 – October 2025. Prior to this, I did a postdoc at Aarhus University from September 2021 – September 2024 working in the research group of Prof. Claudia Strauch. I obtained my PhD in Mathematics at the University of Mannheim in September 2021 under the supervision of Prof. Leif Döring.

Research interests: Generative models, statistics for stochastic processes, transfer learning, data-driven stochastic optimal control, high-dimensional statistical inference, Lévy processes and Markov additive processes.

If you are interested in doing a PhD with me, please get in touch via email.
